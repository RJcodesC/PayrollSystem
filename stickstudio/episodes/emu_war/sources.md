# The Great Emu War (1932) – fact check

| Line | Claim | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | 1932, Australian military operation against emus; failed to stop them | Wikipedia "Emu War"; Britannica "Emu War"; National Geographic "The bizarre story of when Australia went to war with emus—and lost" |
| 2 | Drought-driven emu migration onto wheat farms, Campion district, Western Australia | Britannica |
| 3 | Emus ate/damaged wheat crops and broke fences (gaps let rabbits in) | Wikipedia |
| 5-6 | Major G.P.W. Meredith + two soldiers (Sgt S. McMurray, Gunner J. O'Halloran); two Lewis guns; 10,000 rounds | Wikipedia; Britannica |
| 7 | Emus split into small groups and scattered under fire | Wikipedia; History Hit |
| 8-9 | A gun mounted on a truck; the truck could not keep up / ride too rough to aim | Wikipedia; scienceblog/National Geographic |
| 10 | A large group approached and the gun jammed | Wikipedia |
| 11-12 | Meredith reported 986 kills using 9,860 rounds = ~10 rounds per bird | Wikipedia; Scientific American; Britannica |
| 13 | Meredith: emus "could face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks" (paraphrased "like tanks") | Britannica |
| 14 | Troops withdrawn on 10 December 1932; emus not eradicated | Wikipedia |

Not used: the popular "20,000 emus" figure (weakly sourced) – script says "thousands".
Characters are cartoon stand-ins; the soldiers are not named on screen.
